# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to identify business trends, evaluate profitability, and generate actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL (MySQL):** Data analysis and business queries
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **Excel:** Dataset inspection

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing categories and products.
- Evaluate regional performance.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Analyze shipping performance.
- Identify business improvement opportunities.

## Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 4,000 |
| Total Customers | 672 |
| Total Sales | 386.82 Million |
| Total Profit | 51.42 Million |
| Profit Margin | 13.29% |

## Key Business Insights
1. Sales increased from 74.47 million in 2021 to 120.94 million in 2024.
2. Technology generated the highest category profit.
3. Furniture had a low profit margin of 0.96%.
4. Nairobi generated the highest regional profit.
5. Coast recorded an overall loss of 1.35 million.
6. Higher discount levels were associated with lower average profit.
7. Phones generated the highest sub-category sales and profit.

## Power BI Dashboard
An interactive dashboard was created to monitor:
- Overall business KPIs
- Monthly sales trends
- Category-wise sales and profit
- Region-wise profitability

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights opportunities to improve Furniture profitability, investigate regional losses in Coast, and review discount strategies.
