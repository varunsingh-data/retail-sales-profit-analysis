# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis — Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Business Performance
- Total Sales: 386.82 Million
- Total Profit: 51.42 Million
- Total Orders: 4,000
- Total Customers: 672
- Overall Profit Margin: 13.29%

**Insight:** The business generated 51.42 million in profit with an overall profit margin of 13.29%.

### 2. Yearly Business Growth
- Sales increased from 74.47 million in 2021 to 120.94 million in 2024.
- Profit increased from 8.47 million in 2021 to 17.19 million in 2024.
- Profit margin improved from 11.37% to 14.22%.

**Insight:** Both sales and profit showed consistent yearly growth between 2021 and 2024.

### 3. Category Performance
- Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
- Technology profit margin: 19.32%.
- Office Supplies profit margin: 16.37%.
- Furniture profit margin: Only 0.96%.

**Insight:** Technology is the strongest-performing category, while Furniture needs profitability improvement.

### 4. Regional Performance
- Nairobi generated the highest profit: 21.17 million.
- Rift Valley generated 14.95 million profit.
- Coast recorded a loss of 1.35 million.
- Coast had the highest loss-row percentage at 43.92%.

**Insight:** Coast is the weakest-performing region and requires further investigation.

### 5. Impact of Discounts
- Products with no discount had the highest average profit.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower average profit.
- Discounts of 30% and above showed negative average profit in the overall analysis.

**Insight:** High discounts are associated with reduced profitability. Discount strategies should be reviewed carefully.

### 6. Product Performance
- Phones generated the highest sub-category sales: 114.37 million.
- Phones also generated the highest sub-category profit: 18.80 million.
- Tables and Bookcases recorded overall losses.

**Insight:** Phones and Copiers are strong profit contributors, while Tables and Bookcases need attention.

### 7. Shipping Performance
- Standard Class had the highest average shipping time: 5.51 days.
- Same Day had an average shipping time of 0 days.
- 51.20% of Standard Class rows took more than 5 days to ship.

**Insight:** Standard Class has the longest shipping interval and should be monitored for operational efficiency.

---

## Overall Recommendation

The business should focus on improving Furniture profitability, investigating losses in the Coast region, reviewing high-discount strategies, and maintaining growth in strong categories such as Technology.
